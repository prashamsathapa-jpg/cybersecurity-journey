# Day 4 - Hacker Mindset (Cybersecurity Thinking)

## What I learned today:
Cybersecurity is about thinking like an attacker to defend systems better.

Attackers follow steps:
1. Reconnaissance (gather information)
2. Scanning (find open ports/services)
3. Exploitation (use vulnerabilities)
4. Access & persistence
5. Cover tracks

---

## What I practiced:
- Understood how Nmap output can be used by attackers
- Identified open SSH port (22)
- Thought about system exposure and security risks

---

## Key concept:
Every open port is a possible entry point into a system.

---

## Example:
Port 22 (SSH) is open to allow remote login. If misconfigured, it can be exploited.

---

## Conclusion:
I learned how attackers think during the early stages of an attack and how system scanning helps identify exposed services.
